How to design icons? The most important classes.
execstyleStroke that shows the execution state (passive, active, vote); put it on the outline of the icon.
execstylethinSame as execstyle, but thinner.
colorstyleReceives the per-element style (e.g. a custom colour).
standDefault outline: background fill, dark stroke.
standthin / standfatLike stand, but with a thinner / thicker stroke.
standwithoutBackground fill only, no stroke (e.g. the middle part behind the label).
standlineDark line without fill (e.g. the top and bottom line of the middle part).
normalSolid dark fill, for glyphs.
black / grey / whiteSolid shapes in dark, light grey, or background colour (white covers what is beneath).
labelOn the empty <text> in the middle part; the label is filled in when drawing.
